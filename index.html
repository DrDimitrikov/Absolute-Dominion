W A S D — move / strafe
Hold RMB + move — rotate camera
C — toggle mouse look (ship flies where you look)
F — fire weapons
E — open ship editor
Esc — release mouse lock

SHIP EDITOR

Click a block below, then click the grid to place it.
R rotates the block you're about to place.
Q deselects — click a placed block to select it (highlights yellow), then X deletes it.
E exits and saves your ship.
RMB+move = rotate view  |  R = rotate  |  Q = deselect  |  X = delete  |  E = exit
